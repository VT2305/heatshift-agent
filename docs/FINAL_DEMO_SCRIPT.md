# 90-second final demo script

**0–10 sec — Problem**
“Singapore already knows when heat is rising. The operational problem is what a site supervisor does next across workers, tasks, deadlines and dependencies.”

**10–18 sec — Baseline**
Show the current 31.6°C WBGT, 12 workers and today's schedule.

**18–25 sec — Trigger**
Press **01 · Heat spike**.
“WBGT has now risen to 32.4°C.”

**25–42 sec — Agentic loop**
Point at Agent Activity:
- site state loaded
- heat state loaded
- safety constraints evaluated
- conflict detected
- replacement schedule generated
- validator passed
- operational impact calculated
- approval requested

Say:
“The AI orchestrates the decision. It does not invent the safety rule, and it does not guess the schedule.”

**42–58 sec — Evidence**
Show Before → After and Decision Certificate.
“The optimiser found a lower-disruption candidate; the validator checked skills, conflicts, dependencies and encoded recovery requirements. The certificate records provenance, ruleset version and validation evidence.”

**58–67 sec — Human boundary**
Press **Approve validated plan**.
“The agent cannot approve its own recommendation. A human supervisor remains the final operational authority.”

**67–82 sec — Adaptation**
Press **02 · Worker unavailable**.
“Now a worker becomes unavailable. HeatShift re-enters the loop, checks qualified replacements and replans again.”

**82–90 sec — Close**
“Heat alerts tell supervisors there is a problem. HeatShift turns that signal into a safe, executable next plan.”
